About WAGC Philippines

The World's Largest Amateur Golf Championship

The World Amateur Golfers Championship (WAGC) is one of the most prestigious international amateur golf competitions in the world, held across 46+ countries and governed by the Rules of Golf as approved by the United States Golf Association (USGA).

Now in its 6th season in the Philippines, WAGC is proud to host 36 qualifying tournaments in 2025 — spanning Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao — giving passionate amateur golfers from all corners of the country the chance to compete at the highest level.

The top performers from each regional qualifier advance to the Philippines National Finals, where the best in each division compete for the title of Category Champion. The 5 Category Champions then earn the ultimate honor: representing the Philippines at the WAGC World Finals in Jeju Island, Korea — all expenses paid.

International Competition Compete against players from 46+ countries at the World Finals in Jeju Island, Korea.
USGA-Governed Rules All events follow the official Rules of Golf as approved by the USGA — the same standard as the pros.
5 Handicap Divisions Compete against golfers at your exact skill level — from beginners to low-handicap players.
Represent the Philippines Category Champions wear the Philippine colors on an international stage — a moment like no other.

Why WAGC

What Makes WAGC So Unique

Open to All Skill Levels

WAGC uses 5 divisions based on your Handicap Index Rating, so you compete against players at your exact level. Whether you're a weekend golfer or a seasoned amateur, there's a place for you on the course.

A Pro-Level Experience

Enjoy a 4-day competition across world-class championship courses, using full USGA scoring and format. This is what it feels like to play like the pros — and you earned it.

Play for Your Country

WAGC is one of the few competitions where an amateur golfer can genuinely represent the Philippines on an international stage — going head-to-head against players from over 46 countries worldwide.

All-Expense-Paid World Finals

Category Champions receive a fully sponsored trip to the WAGC World Finals — flights, 5-star accommodations, matching team uniforms, and team bags all included. Win here, and the world is yours.

Global Recognition

Recognized All Across the Globe

The World Amateur Golfers Championship is the largest amateur golf tournament in the world, having been commemorated 22 times across numerous countries. WAGC has earned the support and patronage of some of the most legendary names in the history of golf.

Arnold Palmer

Tournament Patron (Former)

The King himself was a Tournament Patron of WAGC for over a decade, lending the championship a legacy of excellence that endures to this day.

Severiano Ballesteros

Tournament Patron (Former)

Seve Ballesteros, one of the most beloved golfers in history, was a proud Tournament Patron of WAGC for over a decade before his passing.

Jesper Parnevik

Current Tournament Patron

Following Arnold Palmer's passing in 2016, PGA Tour veteran Jesper Parnevik stepped in as Tournament Patron, continuing the tradition of world-class support.

Annika Sorenstam

Honorary Chairman

One of the greatest female golfers of all time, Annika Sorenstam serves as the current Honorary Chairman of WAGC — a testament to the championship's commitment to golf at every level.

Official PGA Tour Partner WAGC is proud to be an official partner of the PGA Tour, the highest level of professional golf in the world.
