    Frequently asked questions

    Can I register on behalf of another golfer?

    Yes. Submit a separate registration for each golfer. Use their own contact information where possible.

    How do I pay?

    Our team will contact you with GCash or bank transfer instructions after registration. Your slot is confirmed only after payment has been verified and accepted.

    Can I choose my flightmates?

    Flights are assigned by the tournament committee. Requests for specific flightmates are not accommodated.

    Do I need a WHS ID?

    You may register without a WHS ID. Eligibility to qualify for the National Finals is subject to the approved tournament rules.

    Can I register on tournament day?

    Late registrations and walk-ins are subject to availability and committee approval.
